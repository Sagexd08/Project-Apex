Limits

Where this was weak, and what we did.

WeaknessWhat we didResultStill open
All data synthetic and clusteredAdded out-of-distribution queries, isotropic data, overlapping clusters and n = 100,0001.04 to 1.40 bits at z = 2, recall at least 0.997. At z = 4 the harder sets read about 3 bits.Real embeddings. Needs a dataset download.
Gate calibrated, not provedMeasured error tails over 20 million pairs. Built a per-vector bound and a deterministic bound.Tails reach 5.2 to 7.1σ. The per-vector bound holds on at least 99.94% of pairs but costs about as much as z = 4. The deterministic bound never misses and prunes almost nothing.A sharper bound, in the style of RaBitQ.
Bits read did not become timeLookup-table kernel with incremental planes, and an optimised exact baseline7× faster than the scalar cascade (first run). 0.61 ms against 0.55 ms for the exact scan; a re-run reversed the sign. Inconclusive.SIMD, and larger n where memory bandwidth dominates.
Concurrency result inconclusiveSeven alternating writer on/off trials with a lookup-table readerMedian P99 ratio 1.21 (1.12 to 1.34). Throughput down 12%. Every trial above 1.Quiet machine, multi-core writer, profiling.
