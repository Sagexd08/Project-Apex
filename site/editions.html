Tenants

Every record belongs to one tenant. A key can only reach its own tenant, and only what it was granted.

Cross-tenant reads come back as not found.

Scope = principal AND key

tenant A key reads BNOT_FOUND
tenant A deletes Bfalse
tenant mismatchFORBIDDEN

Structural

one index per tenant no post-filter to get wrong WAL record without tenant: open fails

LIVE
Tenant isolation and permission scopes enforced in the engine (docs/SECURITY.md).

LIVE
A WAL record with a missing or invalid tenant fails recovery; nothing is defaulted.

IN PROGRESS
Key storage, hashing and rotation sit outside the engine; timing side channels are not covered.
